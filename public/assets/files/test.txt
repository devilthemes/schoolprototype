Objective

The purpose of this assignment is to understand the structure of the solar system by creating a physical or digital model that represents the planets, their order, and their relative positions around the Sun.

Introduction

The solar system consists of the Sun and all celestial bodies that orbit around it, including eight planets, moons, asteroids, and comets. The planets, in order from the Sun, are:

Mercury
Venus
Earth
Mars
Jupiter
Saturn
Uranus
Neptune

Creating a model helps visualize how these planets are arranged and how they differ in size and distance.

Materials Required

(For a physical model)

Thermocol balls / clay / paper balls
Paints or colored paper
Cardboard or chart paper
Glue and scissors
Sticks or thread (to hold planets)
Marker or pen (for labeling)

(For a digital model)

Computer or laptop
Software like PowerPoint, Blender, or any design tool
Procedure
Step 1: Plan the Model

Decide whether you will create a 3D model, 2D chart, or digital version.

Step 2: Create the Sun

Make the Sun the largest object and place it at the center of your model.

Step 3: Make the Planets

Create each planet with different sizes and colors:

Mercury – small and gray
Venus – yellowish
Earth – blue and green
Mars – red
Jupiter – largest with stripes
Saturn – rings around it
Uranus – light blue
Neptune – dark blue
Step 4: Arrange the Planets

Place the planets in correct order from the Sun. Keep approximate spacing between them.

Step 5: Label the Planets

Clearly write the name of each planet.

Step 6: Add Extra Details (Optional)
Asteroid belt (between Mars and Jupiter)
Rings of Saturn
Orbits using circular lines
Observation

While creating the model, we observe:

Planets are arranged in a specific order
Inner planets are smaller and rocky
Outer planets are larger and gaseous
The Sun is the central and largest body
